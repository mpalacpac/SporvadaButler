# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Discord bot ("we", "our", or "the Service") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you invite the bot to your Discord server or interact with its commands.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide core features such as moderation logging, leveling, economy systems, tickets, and regional map setups, the bot stores specific data associated with Discord users and servers:
* **Discord User IDs:** To track individual economy balances, XP/levels, inventory items, warnings, and user-specific configurations.
* **Discord Guild (Server) IDs:** To maintain server-specific settings, welcome messages, logging channels, custom prefixes, and map configurations.
* **Content Interaction Data:** Ticket transcripts, warning reasons, custom command logs, and user-submitted feedback required for server administration and moderation tools.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected data is used exclusively for the operation, maintenance, and improvement of the bot's features within your Discord server:
* Managing in-bot currency, trading, and inventory items.
* Calculating experience points and managing role rewards.
* Tracking moderation actions (bans, kicks, timeouts, and warnings) to help server staff maintain community safety.
* Recording support tickets and maintaining regional map sectors.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All collected data is stored securely in a dedicated PostgreSQL database. Access to the database is restricted to authorized deployment credentials, and reasonable security measures are implemented to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
* **Server Removal:** When the bot is removed from a Discord server, all associated guild configurations, reaction role settings, and channel logs can be purged from the database upon request.
* **User Data Wiping:** Users may request the deletion of their personal bot data (such as economy balances and levels) by using the bot's data wipe features or by contacting the server administration team.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The bot relies on hosting infrastructure (such as Railway) and Discord's API to function. These services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take steps to remove such information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the bot after modifications indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support channels in your community server.
